# Parametric insurance and agricultural/wildfire insurance: Argentina, LatAm, global (research as of 12 Nov 2026)

## Q1. Global parametric insurance market: size, growth, agriculture share, protection gap

### Takeaway
Most market-research firms put the global parametric insurance market at about USD 15–21 B in 2025, growing about 8–13 % a year. Agriculture is the fastest-growing end-user segment but not the largest; corporates are about 50 %. Swiss Re puts the global natural-catastrophe protection gap at USD 424 B in 2025, and Latin America protects only about 8–9 % of its cat exposure.

### Inferences
- For the pitch: "a USD 15–20 B market growing at double digits, with wildfire as the leading insured-loss driver and LatAm at 8–9 % resilience." Use a range and do not quote one vendor's number as fact.
- The "why now" is strong. Satellite wildfire triggers went from niche to large-reinsurer products in 2026 (Liberty/ICEYE), but they cover property in the US and Australia. Nobody in these results covers crop or pasture fire in South America.

### Gaps

- No Munich Re 2025/2026 LatAm figure was retrieved.

## Q2. Argentina agricultural insurance: penetration, insurers, premiums, risks covered, fire, parametric

### Takeaway
Argentine agro insurance is large (about 23.6 M ha, ARS 357,000 M in premiums in fiscal 2024) but it is almost entirely hail: about 98 % of premiums are hail or hail plus add-ons. Multirisk is about 1 %. Parametric products barely appear in SSN statistics. The NOA is nearly uninsured: Salta had only 29,000 ha insured and Salta, Jujuy, Tucumán and Santiago del Estero together had about 2.5 % of insured hectares. Fire is only an add-on to hail ("granizo + adicionales") or a forest-plantation cover.

### Cited Findings
All figures in this list are from the SSN "Encuesta en los Seguros Agropecuario y Forestal – Ejercicio Económico 2024" (fiscal year July 2023–June 2024; IF-2025-107265724-APN-GEYE#SSN, published Sept 2025) — [SSN survey PDF (mirror)](https://www.todoriesgo.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/IF-2025-107308393-ESTUDIOS.pdf)
- **Totals:** premiums of **ARS 357,152 M**, claims paid of ARS 328,437 M, **23.574 M ha** insured, 188,584 policies, 35 insurers.
- **Mix by coverage (share of premiums / hectares):** hail 66.6 % / 51.2 %; hail + add-ons 32.3 % / 47.3 %; multirisk 1.14 % / 1.53 % (360,000 ha). "Granizo + adicionales" may include **fire (incendio)**, replanting, frost, drought, wind, "falta de piso" and excess rain.
- **Number of insurers per coverage (2024):** 23 hail, 23 hail + add-ons, 3 multirisk, **5 fire in forest plantations**. The "Seguro Paramétrico" row went 1 (2020), 2 (2021–2023), **0 (2024)**.
- **Market shares (share of agricultural premiums):** Sancor 20.1 % (36.4 % of hectares), La Segunda 19.5 %, Allianz 13.6 %, La Dulce 6.8 %, Zurich 6.8 %, San Cristóbal 6.6 %, Nación 5.3 %, Mercantil Andina 5.0 %, Federación Patronal 3.8 %, Galicia 3.0 %.
- **Five-year series (constant June-2024 pesos):** premiums ranged from 312,677 M to 372,171 M. Hectares were 21, 21, 21, **19 (2023, drought year)**, then 24 M. Claims rose from ARS 138,138 M (2020) to 328,529 M (2024).
- **Loss ratio:** 88.9 % in 2024, about 14 points lower than the year before. The combined ratio was 121.5 %. Average premium rate was **38.8 ‰ of sum insured (about 3.9 %)**.
- **Forest insurance:** about 0.14 M ha insured, premiums ARS 1,301 M, claims ARS **5,195 M** in 2024 (claims about 4× premiums). Some insurers report forest fire under the "Incendio" line, so it is undercounted.
- **NOA by jurisdiction (agricultural premiums in ARS thousands / thousand ha insured / claims):**
  - Santiago del Estero: 3,889,612 / 358 / 824,011
  - Jujuy: 2,168,882 / 20 / 692,733
  - Tucumán: 94,494 / 172 / 50,357
  - **Salta: 43,422 / 29 / 0**
  - For comparison, Córdoba: 110,304,301 / 10,924
- **Parcel sizes:** 287,866 insured parcels. Most annual-crop parcels are up to 99 ha.

### Inferences
- **NOA insured share:** 29 + 20 + 172 + 358 = about 579,000 ha out of 23.574 M ha, about **2.5 %** of national insured hectares. NOA premiums are about ARS 6,196 M of 355,309 M, about **1.7 %**. Salta, the country's main dry-bean province, is about 0.12 % of insured hectares and had zero claims paid. (Own calculation from SSN Cuadro 5.)
- **Rough USD size:** ARS 357,152 M at about ARS 900/USD (mid-2024) is roughly **USD 400 M a year** in agro premiums nationally. This is my own FX conversion; check the rate before quoting.
- **No standalone crop or pasture fire product exists in SSN statistics.** Fire is a hail add-on or a forest-plantation cover, and forest claims ran about 4× premiums in 2024. That supports the case for an index (satellite-measured) fire product instead of loss-adjusted cover.
- The SSN survey shows **0 insurers reporting parametric in 2024**, while Sancor and Galicia advertise parametric drought products. They are probably reported under other lines, or launched after June 2024. Flag this conflict if asked.

## Q3. Regulator (SSN): parametric rules, sandbox, digital policies, blockchain/crypto

### Takeaway
Since **23 July 2026**, Argentina has a general legal framework for parametric insurance (**SSN Resolution 315/2026**). It requires a named data provider plus a backup, disclosure of basis risk, transparency of the data to the insured, and **payment within 15 days**. Those requirements map almost exactly onto an on-chain, auditable index and escrow. The rules say nothing about blockchain, and no SSN sandbox was found.

### Cited Findings
- Res. SSN 315/2026, published and in force on 23 July 2026, added point 23.8 and an annex to the RGAA. Before it, there was no general definition or regime for parametric insurance — [Marval O'Farrell Mairal, 27 Jul 2026](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665); [Boletín Oficial PDF mirror](https://todoriesgo.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/315-BO.pdf)
- **Definition:** payment is triggered when a predetermined index reaches the agreed value. The insured does not need to prove damage and may collect even with no loss — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665)
- **Scope:** life and personal insurance are excluded. The product must be standalone and cannot be a rider — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665)
- **Index requirements:**
  - The policy must name the index and a national or foreign data provider, plus a backup provider or fallback procedure.
  - It must disclose basis risk and the historical trigger frequency over a statistically representative period.
  - The insurer must give the insured access to the data and the calculation results.
  - A feasibility study must show index–risk correlation by area, production type and critical periods.
  - Source: [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665)
- **Authorization:**
  - SSN must authorize the plan, backed by a study validated by an independent actuary registered with SSN. Other insurers may adopt an approved plan unchanged.
  - **No prior approval is needed when the policyholder is the national, provincial or municipal state, or when the sum insured exceeds 1,000,000 UVA (about USD 1.35 M).** In those cases the insurer only notifies SSN within 30 days.
  - Source: [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665)
- **Payout:** fixed amounts, a single amount or tiers by index intensity, **paid within 15 calendar days of the trigger** — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665)
- **Reporting:** insurers report parametric policies to SSN within 30 days of issue (point 23.8) — [Boletín Oficial mirror](https://todoriesgo.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/315-BO.pdf)
- **Digital and blockchain:** SSN allows policies with digital signature and customer instructions via digital platforms. Argentina has "no integral specific regulation regarding blockchain technology" — [Chambers Fintech 2026](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/fintech-2026/26/)
- **Sandbox:** an academic thesis discusses a possible SSN regulatory sandbox and innovation hub, but no formal SSN sandbox resolution was found — [UdeSA thesis](https://repositorio.udesa.edu.ar/jspui/bitstream/10908/23876/1/%5bP%5d%5bW%5d%20M.%20Der.%20Hennawi%2c%20Leonardo%20Malek.pdf). Brazil's SUSEP runs an insurtech sandbox — [CQCS](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/sandbox-regulatorio-da-susep-como-as-insurtechs-estao-redesenhando-o-mercado-de-seguros-no-brasil/)

### Inferences
- PUNA's design fits Res. 315/2026 almost point by point:
  - NASA FIRMS is a named foreign data provider (fallback: CONAE or VIIRS/MODIS alternatives).
  - Audited computation addresses the requirement to make data and calculations available to the insured.
  - A fixed or tiered payout by burned fraction matches the payout rule.
  - On-chain settlement easily beats the 15-day deadline.
- **Go-to-market lever:** provinces and municipalities as policyholders, or tickets above about USD 1.35 M (e.g., a cooperative or lender pool), skip prior SSN plan approval. This is the fastest legal path.
- The payout must still legally come from a licensed insurer to the insured, in pesos or the agreed currency. A smart contract can serve as the transparent trigger and settlement-instruction layer, but a crypto-denominated payout has no explicit regulatory basis. Frame Avalanche as the audit and trigger rail, not as replacing the insurer.

### Gaps
- The full Res. 315/2026 text was not read directly (the Boletín Oficial link fetched a different resolution). Details come from the Marval summary.
- No SSN statement on crypto or stablecoin indemnity payments was found.

## Q4. Wildfire and fire impact in Argentina and NOA (2020–2026)

### Takeaway
Argentina burned about 1.16 M ha in 2020, 501,000 ha in 2023 and 302,000 ha in 2024 (official DNMF data). About 95 % of fires are human-caused, often fire used to renew pasture. NOA fires in 2025–2026 were intense (Salta's July 2026 zonda wind-driven fire wave burned about 7,000 ha and caused at least one death), and the federal fire budget was cut sharply in 2024. Consolidated NOA and Anta hectare figures were not found.

### Cited Findings
- National burned area (Dirección Nacional de Manejo del Fuego, via FOIA): **2020: 1,158,129 ha; 2023: 500,821 ha; 2024: 301,604 ha**, the lowest in 6 years. - Salta–Jujuy border fires, July 2026: about 1,100 ha — [El Ancasti](https://www.elancasti.com.ar/informacion-general/los-incendios-salta-llegan-jujuy-y-arrasan-hectareas-vegetacion-n512106). - Salta, Oct 2025: about 1,800 ha in Hipólito Yrigoyen and 200 in Colonia Santa Rosa — [Infobae, 9 Oct 2025](https://www.infobae.com/sociedad/2025/10/09/continuan-las-tareas-para-combatir-dos-incendios-de-gran-magnitud-que-se-desataron-hace-semanas-en-salta/)
- Salta Jun–Sep fire season: 620 fire interventions reported — [El Litoral](https://www.ellitoral.com/nacionales/620-intervenciones-incendios-forestales-salta-junio-septiembre_0_n0Kjvg3sCC.amp.html)
- Salta crops: Salta is Argentina's main dry-bean province (2021: 76 % of national bean area, 72 % of production). Its planted area is concentrated in **Anta (51 %)**, General San Martín (21 %) and Rosario de la Frontera (12 %); year and crop scope are unclear in the snippet — [Ministerio de Economía, Salta cadenas de valor](https://argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/salta_-_cadenas_de_valor.pdf)
- National bean area reached a record of about 672,000–679,000 ha in 2025/26 — [Bichos de Campo](https://bichosdecampo.com/las-legumbres-argentinas-van-camino-a-cerrar-una-campana-historica-en-volumen-de-produccion/); [Infocampo](https://www.infocampo.com.ar/?p=589410)

### Inferences
- The NOA fire season (Jun–Oct, dry winter plus zonda) does not overlap with the hail season that existing products cover (spring–summer). That is a structural reason why current policies don't protect against it.
- Human-caused fires (95 %), often from pasture burning on neighbouring land, are the kind of event where loss-adjusted claims get disputed. An objective satellite index removes the "who started it" argument from the payout.

- No economic-loss (USD) estimates for NOA fires were found.
- No sources on sugarcane burning volumes in Tucumán and Jujuy were retrieved.
- No hectare figures were found for fires in Anta specifically. The demo's FIRMS data (Nov 2026) is the primary evidence there.

## Q5. LatAm parametric precedents and payouts

### Takeaway
LatAm has the world's longest-running sovereign parametric schemes: CCRIF (about USD 360 M paid since 2007), Mexico's CADENA/AGROASEMEX and cat bonds, and Peru's SAC, which pays a flat S/ 800 per hectare. Brazil's subsidised rural insurance has collapsed to about 2–3 M ha, and Mapfre plans parametric products there from 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **CCRIF SPC (Caribbean and Central America):** 64 payouts to 17 governments totalling about **USD 268 M** (Jun 2007–Dec 2023) — [CCRIF](https://ccrif.org/about-us). The cumulative total is about **USD 360 M** after the record Hurricane Beryl 2024 payouts — [PreventionWeb](https://www.preventionweb.net/news/hurricane-beryl-record-parametric-payouts-underline-evolution-ccrifs-role-caribbean)
- **Mexico CADENA:**
  - It has parametric (rain and temperature thresholds from yield models), field-assessed and hybrid catastrophic crop insurance. AGROASEMEX pays SAGARPA, which channels funds to farmers — [AGROASEMEX/CAC](https://www.cac.int/sites/default/files/AGROASEMEX.pdf); [World Bank brief](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/124521468287160777/pdf/881000BRI0P1300urance04Pager0Cadena.pdf)
  - In 2012, agricultural insurance covered 11.9 M ha (54 % of area) — [FAO](https://coin.fao.org/coin-static/cms/media/20/13954329605800/cadena.pdf)
  - Mexico's cat bonds reached USD 595 M in 2025 — [El Financiero](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/economia/2025/06/17/bonos-catastroficos-de-mexico-alcanzan-595-mdd-y-alistan-nuevo-seguro-por-lluvias-intensas/). The federal catastrophe insurance was renewed with MXN 10,400 M cover in June 2026 — [El Imparcial](https://www.elimparcial.com/dinero/2026/06/10/quien-paga-los-danos-cuando-ocurre-un-desastre-natural-en-mexico-hacienda-renueva-seguro-millonario-con-cobertura-de-10-mil-400-millones-de-pesos/)
- **Peru SAC:**
  - The state pays 100 % of the premium for vulnerable farmers. ### Inferences
- The regional pattern is that **governments are the anchor buyers** of parametric cover (CCRIF, CADENA, SAC), then layer on farmer-level products. Combined with the Res. 315/2026 exemption for state policyholders, an Argentine province is the most credible first buyer.
- Peru's S/ 800/ha (about USD 210/ha) is a public benchmark for a flat per-hectare catastrophic payout.

### Gaps
- No Colombian agro parametric product data (e.g., Finagro, Bancolombia) was retrieved.
- No CCRIF figure for 2025 was found.
- No LatAm parametric product targeting crop or pasture **fire** was found. Every precedent is drought, rain, hurricane or earthquake.

## Q6. Who buys, and typical premium and indemnity sizes per hectare

### Takeaway
Realistic buyers:
- **Insurers** (Sancor, La Segunda, Allianz, Galicia, which already sell parametric drought cover in NOA provinces) as a white-label index or trigger provider.
- **Provinces and municipalities**, exempt from prior SSN approval under Res. 315/2026, as a macro-cover for small producers.
- **Agro lenders and cooperatives** with portfolios above about USD 1.35 M, also exempt.

Existing benchmarks: about 3.9 % average premium rate on sum insured in Argentina, and flat catastrophic payouts of about USD 200/ha (Peru).

### Cited Findings
- Argentina's average premium rate in 2024 was **38.8 per 1,000 of sum insured** — [SSN survey](https://www.todoriesgo.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/IF-2025-107308393-ESTUDIOS.pdf)
- Average cover cost on annual crops is about $32,000/ha over five years (currency and basis unclear) — [InnovaciónDigital360](https://www.innovaciondigital360.com/agrotech/agrotech-seguros-parametricos-agro-argentina-tecnologia-satelital-2026/)
- Galicia Seguros, owned by Grupo Financiero Galicia (a bank group), lets the client pick the sum insured: direct costs, total costs or expected profit. It sells in Salta, Santiago del Estero and Tucumán — [Forbes Argentina](https://www.forbesargentina.com/negocios/en-ano-duplicaron-polizas-seguro-parametrico-agro-n11497/amp)
- Banks already sell agro insurance: Nación Seguros (Banco Nación group) has 5.3 % and Galicia 3.0 % of agricultural premiums — [SSN survey](https://www.todoriesgo.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/IF-2025-107308393-ESTUDIOS.pdf)
- Res. 315/2026 exempts state policyholders and sums insured above 1 M UVA from prior approval — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/la-ssn-regula-los-seguros-parametricos-17665)
- Peru SAC pays S/ 800/ha flat, with the premium fully state-paid — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-gobierno-activa-seguro-agricola-sac-ante-las-heladas-1015437.aspx)
- Arbol's wildfire parametric product has limits up to USD 10 M per policy (commercial and residential, US) — [Arbol](https://www.arbol.io/post/arbol-launches-new-wildfire-parametric-insurance-product-to-fill-growing-coverage-gap-in-the-western-u-s)
- Mexico's model: AGROASEMEX pays the federal agriculture ministry, which distributes to farmers — [CAC/AGROASEMEX](https://www.cac.int/sites/default/files/AGROASEMEX.pdf)

### Inferences
- An illustrative unit-economics frame, my own and not sourced as a market price: sum insured of USD 300–500/ha (about the direct cost of soy or beans) at a 3–5 % rate gives a **premium of about USD 10–25/ha**. A 135-cell plot like Lote 14 at 37.8 % burned would pay a tiered share of the sum insured. Present this as a scenario, not market data.
- **Who pays first:** (1) Salta province or municipalities as policyholder (state exemption, CCRIF/SAC precedent); (2) Galicia or Sancor as insurer licensing the index; (3) reinsurers (Swiss Re, Munich Re, Liberty Re, which is already doing satellite wildfire) as capacity.
- **Why now:** Res. 315/2026 (July 2026) created the legal regime. Salta had a deadly zonda fire wave in July 2026. Satellite wildfire parametrics went mainstream (Liberty/ICEYE, June 2026). NOA insurance penetration is about 2.5 % of national insured hectares.

### Gaps
- No observed market premium or indemnity per hectare for crop or pasture fire cover in Argentina.
- No public data on agro-lender (Banco Nación, cooperatives such as ACA) insurance requirements for NOA credit.
- No hectare or premium figures for Sancor's TDVI product or Galicia's parametric book.
