**Share of claims that need field inspection.** A numeric percentage was **not found**. Contractually it is effectively 100% for indemnity covers (hail and hail + adicionales):
- Standard hail conditions say that once a claim notice is received, the insurer **sends expert adjusters to inspect the damaged fields**.
- If the insurer does not inspect within **15 days**, the farmer's own verification can stand.
- Verification and adjustment costs are borne by the insurer (Ley de Seguros art. 76).
- Source: La Dulce, Condiciones Generales Granizo, cláusulas 15, 19 and 21: https://extranet.ladulceseguros.com.ar/contrato/1468419136LDRI-3_Condiciones%20Generales%20para%20el%20Seguro%20de%20Granizo.pdf
- Hail plus hail + adicionales = **98.9% of agricultural premiums** in Ej. 2024 (SSN-2024 Cuadro 2). So nearly the whole market is adjusted in the field.

## 3. Cost of claims handling (LAE)

**Argentina:**
- Fee charged by a hail/agro field adjuster per inspection or per hectare: **not found**. Mendoza's provincial adjuster regulation (2026–27) has a fee section (liquidación de honorarios), but the amounts were not visible. It assigns one extra adjuster per 300 ha over 300 ha: https://www.losandes.com.ar/politica/atencion-productores-el-gobierno-implemento-un-nuevo-reglamento-las-tasaciones-danos-los-cultivos-n6001272
- Agro LAE ratio (gastos de liquidación de siniestros): **not found as a separate line**.
- Closest SSN ratios for agro + forestry, Ej. 2024 (SSN-2024, p. 8 and Cuadro 19):
  - Production/acquisition expenses: **13.6%** of premiums.
  - Administrative expenses (gastos de explotación): **12.8%**.
  - Total expenses: **16.4%**.
  - Explotación has been 12–16% every year since 2010.

**International benchmark (USDA federal crop insurance):**
- Admin & operating (A&O) subsidy: **12% to 21.9% of premium**, depending on the plan. It covers agent commissions, loss adjustment, IT and overhead. Example: Revenue Protection $11.10/acre on a $60 premium = 18.5%. Source: CRS IF12189 (2022): https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2022-08-10_IF12189_af5a1f3106ccfc96d2b996a7881c72adb0612534.html
- Catastrophic-coverage loss adjustment reimbursement (CAT LAE): **fixed at 6% of premium**.
- A&O + CAT LAE, 2007–2016: about **USD 1.5 bn/year, about 15% of total premium**.
- Agent commissions are the largest expense, capped at 80% of A&O. RMA does not publish actual loss-adjustment cost.
- Source for the last three points: CRS R45291 (2018): https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/20180820_R45291_3095b79d313bc14861605834b89a5ff5194cb4fb.html
- A&O cap since 2015: about USD 1.283 bn/year (CRS IF12189).
- NAIC or AM Best ULAE/ALAE ratios for the crop line: **not found**.

## 4. Parametric agro traction in Argentina

- Ej. 2023: parametric premiums **ARS 187.8 M** (0.22% of agricultural premiums), **20,000 ha** (0.11%), **81 policies**, 71 claims, **ARS 923.4 M paid**. Two insurers offered it. Source: SSN-2023 Anexo Cuadro 1. **[derived]** Loss ratio about 490% in the drought year; premium ≈ ARS 9,390/ha ≈ **USD 36.6/ha** at 256.70.
- Ej. 2024: parametric = **0**. No insurer reported a parametric cover (2 insurers in 2021–2023, 0 in 2024). Source: SSN-2024 Cuadro 10 and Cuadro 12.
- BCR-2026: multirriesgo and parametric have a "limited and declining" share.
- Sancor Seguros "Sequía Max" (TDVI satellite index): about **110,000 ha** in its first year, versus 160,000 ha of its multirriesgo. The article is dated 03/11/2021: https://bichosdecampo.com/sancor-seguros-lanzo-una-novedosa-cobertura-para-la-sequia-que-se-basa-en-la-informacion-satelital-y-puede-ser-monitoreada-desde-el-celular
- Galicia Seguros: parametric drought policies for soy and maize "doubled" in one year, and the broker's capital rose 104%. No hectares or prices given. Covers Salta, Tucumán, Santiago del Estero, Chaco, Catamarca and others. Dated 05/01/2022: https://www.forbesargentina.com/negocios/en-ano-duplicaron-polizas-seguro-parametrico-agro-n11497
- El Norte: parametric drought cover (S4 index). No volumes found: https://www.lavozdesanjusto.com.ar/el-norte-presenta-el-innovador-seguro-parametrico-de-sequia-una-nueva-forma-de-proteger-los-cultivo-94187
- Córdoba 2026 pilot: **up to 500,000 ha** of soy and maize, more than 800 producers, premium **100% paid by the province**, coinsurance including La Segunda. It is a **yield-threshold multirriesgo** with satellite monitoring, not a pure parametric cover. Published 31/01/2026: https://mercado.com.ar/negocios/cordoba-activa-un-seguro-multirriesgo-agricola-piloto-que-cubre-hasta-500-000-hectareas
- Regulation: SSN **Resolución 315/2026** (July 2026) added parametric insurance to the general insurance regulation, which speeds up product approval. Source: innovaciondigital360 (above). Primary text not checked.
- Current (2025–26) parametric policies, hectares or price per hectare by insurer: **not found**.

## 6. Drought and fire frequency

- **Drought 2022/23:**
  - BCR estimated **50 Mt** of soy, maize and wheat lost: **USD 14.14 bn** for producers, about **USD 19 bn** for the whole economy. https://bichosdecampo.com/llorones-con-razones-como-llego-la-bolsa-de-rosario-a-calcular-que-este-ano-se-perdieron-50-millones-de-toneladas-de-granos-por-la-sequia and https://www.bloomberglinea.com/2023/03/09/cifras-de-la-sequia-historica-en-argentina-dolares-que-no-ingresaran-y-perdidas-en-el-sector/
  - Insured impact: Ej. 2023 loss ratio **103.2%**, combined ratio 157.8% (SSN-2024 Cuadro 19).
  - Insured hectares fell to 18.9 M (SSN-2023).
  - Hail policies do not cover drought, so insured drought losses sat in multirriesgo and parametric covers: ARS 7.2 bn and ARS 3.4 bn paid in Ej. 2023 (constant Jun-2024 ARS; SSN-2024 Cuadro 13).
  - **Insured drought losses vs the economic loss: insured is a tiny fraction (protection gap).**
- **Share of seasons with drought claims:** **not found**. Proxy: in 6 of the 15 ejercicios from 2010 to 2024, the agro loss ratio was ≥ 80% (2012, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2023, 2024) (SSN-2024 Cuadro 19).
- **Burned area, Argentina** (Dirección Nacional de Manejo del Fuego via Chequeado, 13/02/2025): 2020: 1,158,129 ha; 2023: 500,821 ha; 2024: 301,604 ha. Top 2024 provinces were Córdoba (~87k), San Luis (70.7k), La Rioja, Corrientes and Formosa; no NOA province in the top. https://chequeado.com/el-explicador/radiografia-de-los-incendios-cuanta-superficie-se-quemo-en-2024-y-cuales-fueron-las-provincias-mas-afectadas/
- **Northern forest burned, 2025** (Greenpeace satellite estimate, native forest only, Feb 2026): **116,498 ha**. Santiago del Estero 53,325; Formosa 22,715; Chaco 21,520; Salta 18,938. https://www.greenpeace.org/static/planet4-argentina-stateless/2026/02/b30fdf7a-informeanualdeforestacionfinal-gp2025.pdf
- Official yearly burned-area figures for Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán: **not found**. SNMF only publishes monthly reports, e.g. https://argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2018/05/diciembre_2024.pdf
- **Fire as an insured agricultural peril:** Fire on annual crops is usually an "adicional" to hail. Forestry fire insurance is tiny: ARS 1.3 bn premiums and 0.14 M ha in Ej. 2024 (SSN-2024 Cuadro 9).

## Quick anchors for the model

- National agricultural premiums: **about USD 390 M/yr**; 23.6 M ha; 188.6k policies; **about 45.5k paid claims/yr**; **about USD 16.5 premium/ha**; average paid claim ≈ USD 7.9k. All Ej. 2024 [derived USD].
- Loss ratio: 89% (2024), 103% (2023), 15-year average about 73%.
- Admin expenses: 12.8% of premium. USDA benchmark: CAT LAE 6% of premium; A&O 12–22%.
- Concentration: 10 insurers ≈ 90% of premiums; top 3 ≈ 53%.
- NOA + Chaco ≈ 2% of premiums: an expansion market, not a base market.
- Parametric: about 0% in 2024 after a peak of 20k ha in 2023. The new SSN Res. 315/2026 opens the regulatory path.
